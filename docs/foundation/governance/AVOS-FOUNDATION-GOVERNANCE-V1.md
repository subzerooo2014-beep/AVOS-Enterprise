# AVOS Foundation Governance & Conformance Engine V1

## Status

Official Core Foundation.

## Purpose

This engine enforces the mandatory AVOS foundation order and prevents products from skipping directly to implementation.

## Mandatory Order

1. Vision
2. Brand Identity
3. Brand DNA
4. Design System
5. Constitutional Foundation
6. Strategic Foundation
7. Platform Foundation
8. Product Architecture
9. Product Development

## Mandatory Controls

- Product registration
- Stage sequencing
- Evidence submission
- Evidence verification
- Gate evaluation
- Stage completion
- Foundation audit
- Conformance status
- Violation reporting
- Production readiness gates
